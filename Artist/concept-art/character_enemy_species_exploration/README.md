# Character Enemy Species Exploration

Temporary concept explorations for Babel's enemy species redesign.

Current intent:

- move enemies away from normal human silhouettes to reduce kill guilt;
- explore an ancient-civilization-inspired humanoid creature faction;
- borrow broad cartoon-game art principles such as playful linework, lively color, and readable comic silhouettes without copying a specific game;
- keep all images in this folder as exploratory until the user explicitly approves a direction.

Images:

- `char_cyclops_builder_explore_01.png` - first exploratory ancient builder creature with a dominant single eye, comic stone-carrying pose, and warm ancient palette.
- `char_cyclops_builder_explore_02.png` - simplified visual-density pass targeting large shapes and medium details; cleaner than v1, but still has too much small detail in rope segments, toes, face lines, and stone cracks.
- `char_miserable_builder_explore_03.png` - stronger personality pass for a miserable, drained, negative-energy builder species; slumped head, half-closed central eye, long limp arms, and hunched burden read well, but rope strands, toes, hand lines, stone texture, and small surface marks still exceed the intended detail budget.
- `char_tablet_face_species_explore_04a.png` - alternate species option A; a living clay-tablet-faced laborer. Strong Babel/theme connection through the tablet head and record-like face, but the face markings and toes still need simplification.
- `char_hollow_mask_species_explore_04b.png` - alternate species option B; a hollow ceramic-mask-faced laborer. Strong non-human identity and memorable face, but rope, toes, and mask edge details remain too dense.
- `char_clay_stone_species_explore_04c.png` - alternate species option C; a clay-stone burden species with brick growths. Strong body-shape variation and reduced human resemblance, but it drifts away from the thin miserable worker archetype and may suit a heavier enemy class better.
- `char_religious_mask_stout_species_explore_05.png` - religious-mask + squat-body species option using the user-provided stocky proportion reference only for body mass. Strong faction identity through the fused ritual mask and visible tired eyes; stocky silhouette reads well, but toe separations, cuff patterns, and cloth marks still need reduction for the intended large-shape style.
- `char_religious_mask_triangular_species_explore_06.png` - revised religious-mask species option responding to user feedback. Stronger inverted-triangle silhouette, clearer worn-mask construction with visible side strap/rim, and reduced surface clutter. Remaining issues: torso planes are still a bit faceted, and the skirt/arm shapes may need one more simplification pass before becoming a stable anchor.
- `char_greek_mask_weak_laborer_explore_07.png` - ancient-Greek masked human-body weak laborer variant. Tests the idea that the body can be human if the mask removes facial identity. The Greek clothing and tragic mask read clearly, and the miserable/negative-energy expression is strong. Risks: exposed human body and realistic skinny limbs may reintroduce kill-guilt; rope, toes, fingers, and stone burden still exceed the preferred detail budget.
- `char_greek_mask_4head_stonebearer_explore_08a.png` - first 4-head-body pass for the Greek masked laborer. Better than v7 on hand-supported stone carrying, but still too lanky and only one supporting hand reads clearly.
- `char_greek_mask_4head_stonebearer_explore_08b.png` - stricter 4-head-body pass. The compressed body, oversized mask, and short legs are closer to the desired fun proportion; the stone no longer relies on rope. Remaining issue: the rear supporting hand is clear, but the second hand is still obscured, so the two-hand-behind-back support pose needs another refinement if this direction continues.
- `char_greek_mask_modelsheet_explore_09.png` - first standard three-view model sheet for the Greek masked laborer without props or action pose. Front/side/back views make the base silhouette, worn mask strap, hair, tunic, belt, and sandals easier to evaluate. The mask reads as worn and more oppressive than previous versions, but still leans toward gloomy misery rather than clearly evil. Detail density remains high in toes, sandals, small anatomy planes, and garment edges.
- `char_greek_fullhead_mask_modelsheet_explore_10.png` - full-head ancient-Greek ritual helmet-mask model sheet using the user's mask reference only for broad design principles: oversized full-head coverage, segmented plates, thick outline, and central focal emblem. Stronger evil/oppressive identity and better facial de-personalization than v9. Remaining issues: toes, sandals, body anatomy planes, and mask scuffs are still more detailed than the preferred large-shape standard.
- `char_greek_fullhead_mask_grin_modelsheet_explore_11.png` - simplified full-head Greek mask model sheet with a reduced palette, fewer helmet plates, and a broad comic grin. Strongest pass so far for playful enemy readability and reduced kill guilt; the mask now feels like a worn oversized headpiece rather than a detailed helmet. Remaining issues: feet, toes, and sandal bands still carry more small detail than the final large-shape standard should allow.
- `char_greek_theater_mask_modelsheet_explore_12.png` - first pass using the user's real ancient-Greek mask reference for broad features: black theatrical face, white cloth hood, round anxious eyes, and open distressed mouth. Better than v11 on maturity and "not low-age" mood, but cloth folds, toes, sandals, and anatomy lines still add too much small detail.
- `char_greek_theater_mask_clean_modelsheet_explore_13.png` - cleaner version of v12 with fewer cloth folds, stronger large-shape hood, simplified palette, and less toy-like expression. Current best candidate for the real-mask direction; remaining issues are mainly that hands and sandals could still be simplified one more step if this direction becomes the anchor.
- `char_greek_theater_mask_integrated_hood_modelsheet_explore_14.png` - v12-based iteration after user preferred v12 over v13. Removes the brown head strap so the black theatrical mask and white hood read as one slip-on headpiece rather than a tied-on mask. Keeps v12's stronger anxious theatrical expression and body/clothing detail level; remaining issues are the same v12 detail-density concerns in feet, sandals, cloth folds, and exposed anatomy.
